## 2024-07-03 - [Accessibility & AI Feedback]
**Learning:** Form labels must be explicitly linked to inputs using 'id' and 'htmlFor' for Playwright's 'get_by_label' to work and for proper screen reader support. Additionally, adding an artificial delay (1.5s) to simulated AI tasks improves the "perceived effort" and user trust.
**Action:** Always verify label-input associations and use loading states with spinners for async AI simulations.
